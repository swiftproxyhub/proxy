FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. You host it on your own PHP hosting, so there is nothing to pay per use and no third-party proxy subscription.

Why is a site blocked in my country?

Governments and ISPs sometimes block domains at the network level. A web proxy fetches the page from an unrestricted server, so the block doesn't apply to your connection.

Am I anonymous?

The target site sees the proxy server's address, not yours. Your connection to the proxy itself is still visible to your ISP — use it for accessing information, not for anything you need to hide from your network provider.

Will logins work?

For most sites, yes — cookies are kept for your session. Avoid entering banking or other highly sensitive credentials through any proxy.

Some sites look broken. Why?

Very JavaScript-heavy apps sometimes load content in ways a proxy can't rewrite. Most classic websites, articles, docs and forums work fine.
